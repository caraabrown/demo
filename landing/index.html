The part no one talks about

AI screeners were
not built for you.

The algorithms that decide whether your resume reaches a human were trained on data that penalizes exactly the profiles that define senior, nonlinear, and diverse careers. Surfaced surfaces every flag and shows you how to neutralize it.

Not by erasing your history. By presenting it in the language the machines read as qualified.

Age & Date Signals
Graduation years, jobs older than 15 years, numeric year counts — all suppress ranking in HiredScore and Eightfold.
Passive Language
"Supported," "helped," "assisted" score lower than "led," "drove," "owned" on behavioral rubrics — affecting women disproportionately.
Career Gaps
Phenom and Eightfold flag gaps as risk signals regardless of reason — caregiving, health, transition, all treated the same.
School Prestige Weighting
Some screeners apply prestige weighting to institution names. HBCUs, HSIs, and regional universities can score lower — regardless of degree.
Nonlinear Trajectories
Industry switches, lateral moves, and consulting periods read as instability in trajectory models — even when they represent deliberate growth.
